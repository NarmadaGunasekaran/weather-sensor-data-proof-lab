Step 1 · Understand the data

What is inside one weather packet?

One packet can contain several anonymous readings. Before using a specialised compression strategy, we need to understand what each channel most likely represents.

Incoming packet

At first, A, B, C, and D have no meaning attached.

{"station":"weather-01",
"sequence":1,
"channels":{"A":21.2,"B":62.3,
"C":4.8,"D":1013.2}}

Channel discovery

The tool observes each channel over 24 packets. It uses typical value, range, and change over time to make a transparent suggestion.
